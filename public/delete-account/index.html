Account Management

Delete Your NJDrive50 Account

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Step-by-Step

How to Delete Your Account

1
In-App (Fastest) Open NJDrive50 → Settings → Account & Profile → Delete Account. Follow the on-screen confirmation.
2
Email Request Email support@njdrive50.com from your registered address with subject: "Account Deletion Request".
3
Web Request (below) Use the email link at the bottom of this page if you no longer have access to the app.

Data Removal

What Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Teen and parent profile information
  • All driving logs and session records
  • Drive summaries and permit milestones
  • GPS route data and coordinates
  • All app activity stored on our servers
  • Account credentials and authentication data

Legal Retention

Data We May Retain

⚠ Required by Law

Some records may be retained for the minimum period required by applicable law. This does not affect your personal driving data.

  • Payment receipts and billing records (financial compliance)
  • Fraud prevention and security audit logs
  • Records required by applicable state or federal regulations

Partial Deletion

Delete Data Only (Keep Account)

You may request deletion of your driving data only, without closing your account. Use Settings → Account & Profile → Delete My Data inside the app, or visit our dedicated data deletion page below.
