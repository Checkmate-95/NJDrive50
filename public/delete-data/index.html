Data Management

Delete My NJDrive50 Data

Remove specific data from your account without closing it. Requests are processed within 30 days.

How to Request

Ways to Delete Your Data

1
In-App (Fastest) Open NJDrive50 → Settings → Account & Profile → Delete My Data. Select the data you want removed and confirm.
2
Email Request Email support@njdrive50.com from your registered address. Use subject: "Data Deletion Request" and specify which data you want removed.
3
Web Request (below) Use the pre-filled email link at the bottom of this page if you no longer have access to the app.

Eligible Data

Data You Can Request to Delete

In your request, specify one or more of the following:

  • Driving logs and session records
  • Teen or parent profile information
  • Drive summaries and permit milestones
  • GPS route data and coordinates
  • Other app activity stored on our servers

Legal Retention

Data We May Retain

⚠ Required by Law

Some records may be retained for the minimum period required by applicable law, regardless of a data deletion request.

  • Payment receipts and billing records (financial compliance)
  • Fraud prevention and security audit logs
  • Records required by applicable state or federal regulations
