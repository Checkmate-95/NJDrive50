Delete Your NJDrive50 Data

To delete specific data (like driving logs or profile information) without removing your NJDrive50 account, please submit your request by emailing:

admin@njdrive50.com

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

In your request, please specify which data you want deleted, such as:

  • Driving logs
  • Teen or parent profile information
  • Driving summaries and milestones
  • Other app activity stored on our servers

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Some records may be retained for legal or billing compliance (e.g. payment receipts or audit logs) for the minimum period required by law.

To delete your entire account and all associated data, visit our Account Deletion page.
