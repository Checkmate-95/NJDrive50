Selective Data Deletion

Delete Selected NJDrive50 Data

You can request deletion of selected stored data while keeping your NJDrive50 account active. If you want to permanently delete your full account and associated data, use the separate account deletion page.

How to request

Ways to Delete Selected Data

1
In-app request Open NJDrive50 → Settings → Account & Profile → Delete My Data. Choose the data categories you want removed, review the confirmation prompt, and submit your request.
2
Email request Email support@njdrive50.com from your registered email address with the subject line “Selected Data Deletion Request” and describe the data you want deleted.
3
No app access? If you no longer have access to the app, you can still use the email option on this page to request deletion of eligible stored data while keeping your account.

Eligible data

Data You Can Request to Delete

You may request deletion of one or more of the following categories while leaving your account active:

  • Driving logs
  • Active drive and practice-session records
  • Milestones and progress records
  • Uploaded documents and paperwork
  • Generated exports and saved output files

Retention disclosure

What May Be Retained

Limited retention

We may retain limited records only when required for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, or response to valid legal requests. We do not retain deleted driving logs, practice-session data, milestones, uploaded documents, paperwork, or exports for ordinary product use after deletion is completed.

  • Basic security and fraud-prevention audit records, if required
  • Records we are legally required to retain under applicable law
  • Email support correspondence related to your deletion request, if needed for compliance follow-up
