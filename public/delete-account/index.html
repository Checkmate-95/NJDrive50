Delete Your NJDrive50 Account

You can delete your account in two ways:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Web request: Email admin@njdrive50.com or use the form below

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

The following data will be permanently removed:

  • Teen and parent profile information
  • Driving logs
  • Driving summaries and milestones
  • Any app activity stored on our servers

You may also request deletion of your data only, without deleting your account, by specifying this in your request.

Some records may be retained for legal or financial compliance (e.g. payment receipts or audit logs) for the minimum period required by law.

Once processed, you will receive a confirmation email. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
